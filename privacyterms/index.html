Privacy & terms.

Privacy policy

At Salon Polo, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. When you book an appointment, contact us, or communicate with us through our website or messaging services, we may collect basic information such as your name, phone number, email address, and appointment history.

This information is used to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Communicate regarding services or bookings
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Respond to inquiries or customer service requests
  • Improve our services and client experience

If you choose to communicate with us through SMS or messaging, message frequency may vary depending on your interaction with our salon. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Salon Polo does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may be shared with trusted service providers used to operate our business systems, such as booking software or messaging platforms.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message.

Terms of service

By contacting Salon Polo or opting into messaging through our website or booking platform, you agree to receive communications from Salon Polo related to your appointments and salon services. These communications may include:

  • Appointment confirmations
  • Appointment reminders
  • Responses to inquiries
  • Follow-up messages related to services
  • Occasional promotions or updates

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message. For assistance, contact the salon directly.

Salon Polo is not responsible for delays in message delivery caused by mobile carriers or service providers.
