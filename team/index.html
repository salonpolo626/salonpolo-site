Who we are

Dream big, put in the work.

What started as a dream in 2017 has now become a reality. In 2020, Salon Polo opened its doors as a suite in Annapolis, MD, with a vision to offer exceptional service and build lasting relationships. While no one could have anticipated the challenges of a global pandemic, it only strengthened our commitment to providing the care and respect every guest deserves.

In September 2024, we proudly opened Salon Polo at our new Annapolis location, 626 Admiral Drive, Suite E, with a mission to create meaningful employment opportunities for like-minded professionals who share our passion for helping others and providing the best service in the industry.

Your trust and support mean everything to us. We deeply value the personal connections we build with every guest, and our team is here to make sure you feel like family from the moment you walk through our doors.
