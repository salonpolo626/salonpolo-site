Booking policy

Cancellation policy.

Canceling and rescheduling

At Salon Polo, we value your time and the time of our staff and other guests. If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please do so at least 24 hours before your scheduled time. You can easily make changes using the link provided in your confirmation email and text or by calling our salon at (443) 949-9786.

No-call, no-show policy

After two instances of a no-call, no-show, you will no longer be eligible to book future appointments with Salon Polo.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through our booking page, where you can choose your stylist or barber and see available times, or call us at (443) 949-9786.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment, using the link in your confirmation email and text or by calling (443) 949-9786. After two no-call, no-shows, you will no longer be able to book future appointments.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Friday, 10 AM to 7 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 2 PM. We're closed Sunday and Monday.

Where are you located?

Salon Polo & Barbershop is at 626 Admiral Drive, Suite E, Annapolis, MD 21401.

Who does hot towel shaves?

Hot towel shaves and head shaves are done by Colette, our licensed barber.

What products do you use?

We use and carry Kevin Murphy hair and grooming products.
