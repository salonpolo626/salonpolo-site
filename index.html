From the chair

One room. Two crafts. No compromise on either.

Salon Polo opened in 2020 with a simple idea: a proper salon and a proper barbershop should be able to share a floor. A blowout and a hot-towel shave deserve the same attention, and so does a kid's first real haircut.

We keep it to four chairs on purpose. You get time, a conversation, and a stylist who knows your hair by the second visit. We work with Kevin Murphy color and care, and we'll send you home with exactly what you need, nothing more.

Henry, owner & stylist
