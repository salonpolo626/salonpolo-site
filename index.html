From the chair

The art of a proper cut.

I opened Salon Polo in 2020 because I wanted a shop where a men's cut got real attention. Not a quick buzz and out the door, but scissor work shaped to how your hair grows, fades blended clean, and a hot towel shave that takes the time it should. We cut women's hair too, with the same care.

There are four chairs here, and that's deliberate. Nobody's rushing to the next client, you get a real conversation, and by your second visit your stylist or barber knows your hair. We use Kevin Murphy products, and we'll only send you home with what you'll actually use.

Henry, owner & stylist
